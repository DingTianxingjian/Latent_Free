## Controlled results (same data / seed / steps / decoder; held-out episodes; single seed unless stated)

**Stage I — one temporal mode preserves the action's shape** (R² of ridge probes from z; corrected R² convention 09-09)

| code | dims | net xyz | first segment | shape (profile) | full sequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mean-pooled, 1 ep | 128 | **0.336** | 0.210 | 0.017 | 0.200 |
| **DC+AC1, 1 ep** | 256 | 0.324 | **0.264** | **0.085** | **0.224** |
| pair-wise tokenizer (UniVLA's unit), pilot | 256 | 0.226 | 0.193 | 0.083 | 0.177 |
| DC+AC1, pilot | 256 | 0.301 | 0.254 | 0.076 | 0.213 |
| mean-pooled, pilot | 128 | 0.332 | 0.212 | 0.016 | 0.190 |

Reading: pooling keeps net displacement and destroys the trajectory shape (profile R² 0.017); one AC mode recovers it (5× on profile, +0.05 on the first segment) at −0.012 net. Using the same architecture pair-wise loses on every target.

**Controlled UniVLA comparison (09-11): UniVLA stage-2, their recipe, our data, our budget (128k × 64):** net 0.236 / seg1 0.222 / prof 0.125 / seq 0.215, patch drop 22.9%, nuisance leak −0.03. ACDC beats it on net (+0.09), seg1 (+0.04), seq (+0.01) at 256 vs 2048 dims; UniVLA's per-pair VQ code keeps more shape (prof 0.125 vs 0.085) and is cleaner of nuisance (leak −0.03 vs +0.11). Their released weights (their data + Ego4D, 8 GPU × 20 epochs): 0.288 / 0.281 / 0.152 / 0.259.

**Stage III — LATP: the transport gain lives in the decoder** (independent reader fitted on real frozen features only)

| arm | transfer R² | transfer cosine | clean R² |
|---|---|---|---|
| tokenizer alone (`s1_acdc`) | 0.212 | 0.465 | 0.301 |
| decoder-only, no swap terms (control) | 0.220 | 0.460 | 0.301 |
| **LATP: frozen tokenizer + swap realism + cycle** | **0.311** | **0.511** | 0.301 |
| swap with encoder fine-tuned | 0.318 | 0.517 | 0.305 |
| legacy tokenizer, 3 seeds of LATP | 0.310 / 0.306 / 0.304 | 0.512 / 0.493 / 0.496 | 0.298 |
| **1-epoch tokenizer → 1-epoch LATP** (`s1_acdc_1ep` → `s3_latp_1ep`) | 0.244 → **0.319** | 0.484 → **0.524** | 0.324 (both) |

Reading: +0.10 transfer R² / +0.05 cosine from the swap objective, replicated on two independent tokenizers and three decoder seeds; zero cost to the clean number because the tokenizer is frozen. Fine-tuning the encoder is not better (Δ ≤ 0.007, within seed spread) — LATP is a modularity/compute statement.

**Stage II — TC/TI by intervention: null at pilot scale** (patch-motion R² in z: 0.14 untreated → 0.17 with both terms; u 8-d reads 0). Redesign (u 64-d) queued. UniVLA's deployed code reads −0.02 on this nuisance while its TI branch is dead: their invariance comes from the quantiser, not the TI head.
